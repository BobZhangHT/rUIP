## Sources prioritized

Publisher DOI pages, PubMed/PMC records, journal pages, and author-hosted or repository copies were used for identity and scope verification. Search snippets alone were not treated as evidence for detailed method claims.

## Screening rules

Included work had to contribute directly to at least one of four clusters: interval-censored survival likelihood/inference, Bayesian data augmentation, historical-information borrowing, or unit-information priors. Application-only papers without a reusable methodological contribution and non-primary summaries were excluded from the final evidence table.

## Remaining uncertainties

- The proof-of-concept `IC-CP` is a summary-normal comparator, not a code-level reproduction of Fang et al. (2025).
- A broader systematic review may uncover disease-specific interval-censored power-prior applications.
- Final manuscript claims should be rechecked immediately before submission because this is a rapidly developing area.
